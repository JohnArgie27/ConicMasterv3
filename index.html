1. The Circle (Complete Solutions)

Formulas: Origin: x^2 + y^2 = r^2  |  Translated: (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2
Example 1: Standard Equation at Origin

Problem: Find the standard equation of a circle with center at (0,0) and radius r = 5.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Use origin formula: x^2 + y^2 = r^2
2. Substitute r = 5: x^2 + y^2 = 5^2
Final Answer: x^2 + y^2 = 25

Example 2: Translated Center Equation

Problem: Find the equation of a circle with center at (2, -3) and radius r = 4.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Use translated formula: (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2
2. Substitute h = 2, k = -3, r = 4: (x - 2)^2 + (y - (-3))^2 = 4^2
Final Answer: (x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = 16

Example 3: Extracting Radius from Equation

Problem: Find the radius of the circle given by x^2 + y^2 = 49.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Compare with x^2 + y^2 = r^2
2. Thus, r^2 = 49 implies r = sqrt(49) = 7
Final Answer: Radius r = 7

Example 4: Circle with Radical Radius

Problem: Write the equation of a circle with center (-1, 5) and radius r = sqrt(3).

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Substitute h = -1, k = 5, r = sqrt(3) into (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2
2. (x - (-1))^2 + (y - 5)^2 = (sqrt(3))^2
Final Answer: (x + 1)^2 + (y - 5)^2 = 3

Example 5: Identifying Center Coordinates

Problem: Find the center (h, k) of the circle (x - 3)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 81.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Match (x - 3) with (x - h) implies h = 3
2. Match (y + 2) with (y - k) implies y - (-2) implies k = -2
Final Answer: Center is (3, -2)

2. The Parabola (Complete Solutions)

Formulas: Origin: y^2 = 4px  |  Translated: (y - k)^2 = 4p(x - h)
Example 1: Finding Focal Parameter p

Problem: Find the focal length parameter p for the parabola y^2 = 12x.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Compare y^2 = 12x with standard form y^2 = 4px
2. Set 4p = 12 implies p = 3
Final Answer: p = 3

Example 2: Writing Translated Parabola Equation

Problem: Find the equation of a horizontal parabola with vertex (-2, 1) and p = 2.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Use (y - k)^2 = 4p(x - h). Here h = -2, k = 1, p = 2.
2. (y - 1)^2 = 4(2)(x - (-2)) implies (y - 1)^2 = 8(x + 2)
Final Answer: (y - 1)^2 = 8(x + 2)
